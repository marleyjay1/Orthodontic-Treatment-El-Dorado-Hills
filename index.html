What Is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment focuses on correcting problems involving the position of the teeth and the alignment of the jaws. Common concerns include crowded teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites, crossbites, open bites, and other alignment issues.

Modern orthodontics offers several treatment options, allowing patients to choose an approach that fits their age, dental needs, lifestyle, and smile goals. A professional orthodontic evaluation can determine which option is most suitable.

Common Orthodontic Problems

People seek orthodontic treatment for many different reasons. Some concerns are primarily cosmetic, while others can affect the way the teeth function together.

Crowded Teeth

Crowding happens when there is not enough space for teeth to fit properly. Overlapping teeth can make brushing and flossing more difficult. Orthodontic treatment can gradually move teeth into more organized positions.

Spacing and Gaps

Gaps between teeth may occur naturally or develop because of missing teeth, tooth size, or other factors. Orthodontic treatment can help close or reduce unwanted spaces when appropriate.

Overbite

An overbite occurs when the upper front teeth extend too far over the lower front teeth. Depending on the severity and underlying cause, orthodontic treatment may help improve the relationship between the upper and lower teeth.

Underbite

An underbite occurs when the lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth when the mouth is closed. Early evaluation can be particularly useful for growing children and teenagers because treatment planning can consider jaw development.

A crossbite occurs when some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth rather than outside them. An open bite occurs when certain upper and lower teeth do not meet properly when the mouth is closed. Orthodontic care may help improve these bite relationships.

Orthodontic Treatment Options in El Dorado Hills

Orthodontic treatment is no longer limited to traditional metal braces. Depending on your specific needs, your orthodontic provider may recommend braces, clear aligners, or other orthodontic appliances.

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces use brackets and orthodontic wires to apply controlled pressure to the teeth. They remain a reliable option for many children, teenagers, and adults.

Clear Braces

Clear or ceramic braces can provide an alternative to traditional metal brackets. They are designed to be less noticeable while still providing orthodontic correction for suitable patients.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable trays designed to gradually move teeth. They are particularly appealing to patients who want a more discreet orthodontic option.

Why Choose Personalized Orthodontic Care?

Every smile is different. Two patients may have similar-looking crooked teeth but require completely different treatment plans because of differences in bite, tooth position, jaw structure, or overall oral health.

A personalized approach can consider:

  • Your current tooth alignment
  • Bite relationship
  • Jaw development and position
  • Age and dental development
  • Treatment goals
  • Lifestyle preferences
  • Previous orthodontic treatment
  • The type of correction required

This is why an orthodontic examination should come before deciding whether braces or clear aligners are right for you.

Your Orthodontic Journey

The orthodontic process generally begins with a consultation and examination. Your orthodontic provider evaluates your teeth and bite and discusses your concerns and goals.

If treatment is recommended, a customized plan is developed. Depending on the treatment method, you may have regular appointments so your orthodontic provider can monitor progress and make adjustments.

After active treatment is completed, retainers are commonly used to help maintain the new tooth positions. Following your orthodontist's retention instructions is an important part of protecting your results.
